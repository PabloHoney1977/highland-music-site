Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to all Highland Music LLC apps

Highland Music LLC ("we," "us") builds music-learning apps for iOS, including Jazz Guitar Lab, Piano Chords Lab, and Note Quest (the "Apps"). We care about your privacy and keep the amount of data we handle to a minimum. This policy explains what we collect and why.

The short version

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, or location.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the Apps are used in aggregate, and Apple/RevenueCat to process in-app purchases.
  • Our apps for children — including Note Quest — contain no analytics at all and no advertising.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress, streaks, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device (for example, in the app's local storage). This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. Deleting the App removes it.

Analytics

We use analytics tooling (PostHog) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data — such as which features are opened and general app events — so we can improve the Apps. This data is not used to identify you personally and is not linked to your name or contact details.

Exception for apps directed to children. Note Quest is designed for children and includes no analytics of any kind — in any version, on any platform. It loads no analytics software and transmits no usage data, no advertising identifier (IDFA), and no device identifiers to us or to any third party.

In-app purchases

Our Apps offer a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features. Purchases are processed by Apple, and we use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and validate receipts. We never see or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple. Apple and RevenueCat may process a purchase identifier to deliver and restore your purchase.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Note Quest is an educational game designed for children, including children under 13. Jazz Guitar Lab and Piano Chords Lab are general-audience tools and are not directed to children under 13.

We have designed Note Quest so that it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Specifically, in Note Quest we do not:

  • ask for a name, email address, birthdate, phone number, photo, or any other personal detail;
  • require or offer an account, login, or user profile;
  • collect location data;
  • include any analytics, advertising, tracking, or social-media integration;
  • include chat, messaging, user-generated content, or any way for a child to communicate with others;
  • transmit personally identifiable information or device information to third parties.

A child’s score, streak, stickers and settings are saved only on the device and are never sent to us. Because we collect no personal information from children, we do not need to obtain verifiable parental consent under the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”), and there is no child data for a parent to review or delete from our systems — deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Purchases and external links are behind a parental gate. The only in-app purchase, and the only links that leave the app (including the links to this policy and to support), are placed behind a parental gate that requires solving a written arithmetic question — so a young child cannot make a purchase or leave the app on their own. The purchase itself is processed by Apple and managed by RevenueCat (see In-app purchases above); RevenueCat receives only an anonymous, randomly generated purchase identifier, never a name, email address, or advertising identifier.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, or you have any question about children’s privacy in our apps, email support@highlandmusicapps.com and we will respond promptly.

Third-party services

The Apps rely on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Apple (App Store & in-app purchases)
  • RevenueCat (purchase management)
  • PostHog (anonymous product analytics) — not used in Note Quest

Your choices

You can stop all data collection by deleting the App. You may also request information about the limited data associated with your device by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact
